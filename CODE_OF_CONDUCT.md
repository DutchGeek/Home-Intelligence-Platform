# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard
Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate clearly, respectfully, and professionally.

## Expected Behavior
- Focus on facts and reproducible behavior.
- Assume good intent while holding a high technical bar.
- Give direct, respectful feedback.
- Keep production safety and user impact in view.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Deliberate disruption of constructive technical discussion

## Enforcement
The repository owner may remove content, restrict participation, or take other moderation action when needed to protect the project and its contributors.
